This decision would entail granting one restaurant a monopoly in what used to be a three-restaurant competitive space, which is bound to raise prices. 

Prices may not rise in the short term, but as soon as Pita & Olive become a regular, seemingly irreplaceable part of the union, they would have nothing stopping them from raising prices other than a Student Senate that can do little more than "advocate for reasonable pricing".



In order to solve this problem, the Student Senate must be given real power over the pricing.

My proposition would be indefinite price controls on all Pita & Olive menu items, set unilaterally by the Student Senate.

This would ensure that the prices remain fair—not through the goodwill of a private corporation, but through real usable that the Student Senate could actually use to force them to keep their prices low.
